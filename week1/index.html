McAllen, Texas

McAllen is a city in Hidalgo County, Texas, located in the Rio Grande Valley along the US-Mexico border. It is one of the fastest-growing cities in the United States and is known for its warm climate, vibrant Hispanic culture, and strong sense of community. We are best known for our hot climate, usually hitting the 100s in the summer, and our delicious Tex-Mex cuisine. We have a rich cultural heritage with a blend of Mexican and American influences, which is reflected in our food, music, and festivals. McAllen is also home to many parks, museums, and shopping centers, making it a popular destination for winter texans.
